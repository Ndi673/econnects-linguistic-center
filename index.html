ABOUT THE CENTER

Empowering Learners Through Language Education

CONNECTS Excellent Services – Linguistic Center provides language training and examination preparation opportunities for learners at different proficiency levels.

Our programmes include intensive English courses, international examination preparation and German language instruction, with flexible online and on-site learning options.

Located at Petrolex Ndobo, Douala, we welcome beginners, intermediate learners and advanced students.

Our Learning Approach

  • Practical language development
  • Structured learning levels
  • Examination preparation
  • Flexible learning arrangements
  • Individual learner support
